Something remains constant.

The Soul Reveal

If everything external is removed… who is still there?

The body changed, the feelings passed, the title was retired, the name was just a label, the profile was just a projection, even the thoughts kept shifting. Underneath all six, something radiant was watching the whole time — untouched, unchanged, and still here.

"As the embodied soul continuously passes, in this body, from boyhood to youth to old age, the soul similarly passes into another body at death. A sober person is not bewildered by such a change."
— Bhagavad Gita 2.13

The Eternal Anchor

Which happiness survives life's ups and downs?

External highs rise with pleasant circumstances and plummet when life turns difficult. But the joy of spiritual connection (Atma-rati) does not depend on changing conditions — it remains eternally serene, whole, and unshakable.

"Yam labdhvā cāparaṁ lābhaṁ manyate nādhikaṁ tataḥ — Upon gaining this spiritual connection, one considers no other gain greater, and standing in such a position, one is never shaken, even amidst the greatest difficulty."
— Bhagavad Gita 6.22
